## What this book covers

How matter becomes useful. Metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, semiconductors. How atomic structure determines bulk properties (strength, conductivity, transparency), and how humans design and process materials to get the properties they want.

## Why this book exists

Civilizations are often named by their dominant material (Stone Age, Bronze, Iron, Silicon). Materials are the substrate of every object you own. The old tree buried materials inside `HA_chemistry` and `HA_technology` where it lost its distinct engineering character.
