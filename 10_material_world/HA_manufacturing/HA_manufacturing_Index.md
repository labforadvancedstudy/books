## What this book covers

How humans make things in large numbers. Factories, assembly lines, supply chains, lean manufacturing, quality systems, robotics, and the economics of scaling from prototype to a million units.

## Why this book exists

Design is easy. Making one is medium-hard. Making a million identical ones with acceptable quality at acceptable cost is an entire discipline that took humanity 300 years to figure out. The old tree had no home for this.
