## What this book covers

Logistics and mobility at civilization scale: roads, rail, shipping, aviation, pipelines, and urban transit. How goods and people move, what determines where transport networks grow, and how the whole system integrates.

## Why this book exists

Transport is hiding inside HA_technology in the old tree. But transport is a civilizational layer of its own: it determines supply chains (sec 10), where cities grow (sec 9 HA_geography), and how people spend their days.
