## What this book covers

How humans get 8 billion people fed. Crops, livestock, aquaculture, soil, agronomy, fertilizer, irrigation, pesticides, the Green Revolution, food logistics, and the dependencies this whole system sits on.

## Why this book exists

Agriculture feeds civilization. The Haber-Bosch process alone — synthesizing ammonia for fertilizer — is credited with sustaining approximately half the world's current population. That is the single most consequential chemical process ever industrialized, and the old tree had nowhere to put it.
