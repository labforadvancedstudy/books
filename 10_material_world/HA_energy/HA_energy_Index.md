## What this book covers

How humans generate, move, store, and consume energy at civilization scale. Electricity generation (fossil, nuclear, renewable), liquid fuels, natural gas, heating, batteries, the grid, and the energy transition.

## Why this book exists

Energy is a subset of physics (covered in `2_physical_emergence/HA_energy/`) AND a civilizational infrastructure (covered here). The physics book handles "what is energy." This book handles "how does society run on it." Phase 2C will rebalance the split.
