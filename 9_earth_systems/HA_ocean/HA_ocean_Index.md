## What this book covers

The world ocean treated as a single connected system: physical oceanography (currents, temperature, salinity), chemical oceanography (carbon, nutrients, acidification), biological oceanography (plankton to apex predators), ocean-atmosphere coupling, and the human interface (fisheries, shipping, pollution).

## Why this book exists

70% of Earth's surface, 97% of Earth's water, the majority of its biosphere by volume — and the old tree had no dedicated book on it. The ocean regulates climate, feeds billions of people, and is the least-mapped region of the solar system.
