## What this book covers

Fresh water on and under Earth's surface: rivers, lakes, groundwater, glaciers, the hydrologic cycle, watershed ecology, water supply engineering, and water stress. Distinct from `HA_ocean/` (saltwater) and `HA_climate/` (atmosphere as a whole).

## Why this book exists

Water is the one resource nothing substitutes for, and civilization-scale water systems are chronically invisible until they fail. A dedicated book prevents hydrology from being hidden inside either climate or civil engineering.
