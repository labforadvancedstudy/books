## What this book covers

The ground beneath your feet, treated as a layered dynamic system. Rocks, minerals, plate tectonics, volcanism, earthquakes, deep-time history of Earth's crust and mantle.

Distinct from `HA_climate/` (atmosphere), `HA_ocean/` (water), `HA_hydrology/` (fresh water). This book covers the lithosphere and what lies beneath.

## Why this book exists

The original tree handled geology in passing within `2_physical_emergence/` but never as its own object of inquiry. A planet's solid geology is where life evolved, where civilizations rose and fell on top of it, and where most of the resources the `10_material_world/` domain consumes come from. It deserves a dedicated HA_*.
