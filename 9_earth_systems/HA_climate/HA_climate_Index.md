## What this book covers

The atmosphere as a dynamic system: daily weather, long-term climate, energy balance, feedback loops, human forcing. Includes paleoclimate evidence and the physics of the greenhouse effect.

Distinct from `HA_geology/` (solid earth) and `HA_ocean/` (hydrosphere). This book covers the gaseous envelope and everything weather-and-climate does in it.

## Why this book exists

Climate is arguably the most politically charged domain of applied science. A dedicated book with strict tier discipline matters here more than elsewhere: L1-L7 is solid physics and observation, L8 is policy and attribution (where reasonable people disagree on framing, not on facts), L9 is where speculation lives and must be labeled.
