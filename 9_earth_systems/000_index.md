## What this section covers

The planet itself as a system: solid Earth, atmosphere, oceans, climate. Distinct from `2_physical_emergence` (fundamental physics) and `3_biological_emergence` (life), Earth systems science studies the **specific layered dynamics of this one planet** over geological and historical timescales.

This section was missing from the original tree. Creating it explicitly so the gap is visible and fillable.

## Why this exists as a separate top-level

Earth systems content does not fit cleanly under emergence-level headings:
- It is **post-physical-emergence** (atoms exist) but **pre-biological** in some aspects, co-emerging in others.
- It is **pre-civilizational** as substrate, yet civilization-scale problems live here.
- Forcing it into `2_physical_emergence/HA_earth_systems/` would hide its scope.

## Evidence tier philosophy for this section

- L1-L5: **[Textbook]** — solid earth / climate / ocean science is mature.
- L6-L8: **[Cross-disciplinary]** — policy, economics, societal response.
- L9: **[Speculative]** — deep-time projections, anthropocene boundaries.
