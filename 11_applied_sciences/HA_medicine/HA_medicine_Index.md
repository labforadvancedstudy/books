## What this book covers

How humans diagnose and treat disease. Not biology per se (that lives in `3_biological_emergence/HA_life/`), but the practice of medicine as a profession and a body of applied knowledge: anatomy, physiology as used at the bedside, diagnosis, pharmacology, surgery, evidence-based medicine, medical specialties.

## Why this book exists

The old tree had no medicine. Anywhere. A collection claiming to be "a complete map of knowledge" cannot skip the single field humans interact with most urgently across their lifetime.
