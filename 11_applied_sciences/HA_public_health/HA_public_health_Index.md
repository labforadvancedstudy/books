## What this book covers

Health at population scale: epidemiology, sanitation, vaccination, health policy, outbreak response, non-communicable disease prevention, health equity. The complement to clinical medicine — not treating one sick person, but keeping populations healthy.

## Why this book exists

Clean water saves more lives than clinical medicine. Vaccination saves more than surgery. Public health is quietly the largest life-saving enterprise in history. The old tree had no place for it.
