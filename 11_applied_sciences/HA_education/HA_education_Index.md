## What this book covers

How humans formally teach and learn. Pedagogy, learning science, curriculum design, school systems, higher education, lifelong learning, educational assessment, and the policy questions that determine who gets what education.

## Why this book exists

Education occupies most of a person's first two decades and shapes the rest. It is one of the largest institutional enterprises on earth (hundreds of millions of teachers, billions of students) and was entirely absent from the old tree.
