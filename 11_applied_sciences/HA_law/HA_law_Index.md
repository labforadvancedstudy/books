## What this book covers

Law as a system of rules, institutions, and reasoning that governs human conduct. Common law and civil law traditions, constitutional structure, major branches (criminal, civil, administrative, international), and how law is actually made and interpreted.

## Why this book exists

Law is a silently powerful system that shapes everything from what you can say in public to who inherits your property. It was entirely absent from the old tree, which is a large gap given how much of daily life is either permitted, forbidden, or regulated.
