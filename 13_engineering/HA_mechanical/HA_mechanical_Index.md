## What this book covers

The design of systems that move, bear loads, convert energy, and transfer heat. Statics, dynamics, strength of materials, thermodynamics as applied, fluid mechanics, machine design, vibration, and control.

## Why this book exists

Mechanical engineering is the oldest engineering discipline still in continuous practice — the Romans and Greeks had early versions. It designs almost every physical object you use. The old tree had no home for it.
