## What this book covers

The design of buildings, bridges, roads, water systems, and the urban environment. The structural engineering and the architectural disciplines — treated together because in practice they are inseparable, even when organized into different professions.

## Why this book exists

Everybody lives inside structures designed by civil engineers and architects. These disciplines are extremely mature and accumulated; treating them as footnotes to "technology" distorts them.
