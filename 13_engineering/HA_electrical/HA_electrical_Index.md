## What this book covers

The design of systems that use electricity and electromagnetism to do work, move information, or sense the world. Power systems, signals, circuits, electronics, control, radio, and the interface with digital computing.

## Why this book exists

Electrical engineering is the discipline that enables the grid (HA_energy), computers (HA_computer), communication (HA_internet), and most modern sensors. Without a dedicated book, these topics are scattered and their shared foundation is invisible.
