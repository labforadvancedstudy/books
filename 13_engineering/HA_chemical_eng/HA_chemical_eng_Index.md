## What this book covers

The design and operation of industrial-scale chemical processes: refining, synthesis, separation, reaction engineering, transport phenomena, process control, and scale-up from lab to plant.

## Why this book exists

Chemical engineering makes everything from fuels to plastics to pharmaceuticals to fertilizer to semiconductors' cleaning chemistries. It is quietly enormous (chemicals are roughly 10% of global GDP) and was invisible in the old tree.
