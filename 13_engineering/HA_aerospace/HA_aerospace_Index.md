## What this book covers

The design of vehicles that fly in atmosphere (aircraft) or outside it (spacecraft). Aerodynamics, propulsion, structures for flight, flight controls, orbital mechanics, and the engineering of complex mission systems.

## Why this book exists

Aerospace is separate from mechanical engineering because its constraints (weight, reliability, aerodynamic regimes, space vacuum) are different and drive different design choices. It also overlaps with `8_cosmic_futures/HA_space_engineering/` — this book covers practiced aerospace; that one covers speculative megastructures.
