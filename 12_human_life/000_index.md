## What this section covers

The lived human experience at the scale of a single body and a single lifetime. Birth, growth, sex, pain, illness, love, aging, death.

The original tree had detailed chapters on quarks, galaxies, and civilizations, but no place for childbirth, puberty, menopause, chronic pain, or dying. It treated humans as cognitive agents (psychology) or social agents (society) but not as embodied, mortal, developing creatures. That is the gap this section closes.

This is **not** a duplicate of `3_biological_emergence/HA_life/` or `HA_consciousness/`. Those cover *life as biological phenomenon* and *consciousness as emergent property*. This section covers *what it is to be a human body from cradle to grave*.

## Why this exists as a separate top-level

- The existing tree's treatment of humans was disembodied: `HA_psychology` = cognitive architecture, `HA_consciousness` = qualia, `HA_society` = groups. Nobody was living in a body.
- These topics unify biology, medicine, culture, and phenomenology in a way no single existing section can hold.
- They also attract extreme Speculative/ideological noise (aging reversal, gender theory, near-death experiences). Having a dedicated home means tier discipline can be applied consistently instead of scattered across essays.

## Evidence tier philosophy for this section

- L0-L3: **[Textbook]** — anatomy, developmental biology, endocrinology, gerontology.
- L4-L6: **[Cross-disciplinary]** — developmental psychology, medical sociology, thanatology.
- L7-L8: **[Cross-disciplinary / Speculative]** — meaning of mortality, gender theory debates, ethics of enhancement.
- L9: **[Speculative]** — radical life extension, post-gender futures, digital immortality.
- Explicit rule: no Speculative claim escapes L8 without a tier marker. This section has the highest motivated-reasoning risk in the tree.
