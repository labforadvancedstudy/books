## What this book covers

Human sex, gender, and sexuality as lived experience and as scientific subject. Biological sex, gender as social and personal identity, sexual orientation, sexual behavior, reproduction, and the places where biology and culture are inseparable.

Explicit rule: this domain has very high motivated-reasoning density on all sides. Tier discipline matters especially. The book will distinguish clearly between:
- Textbook-level biology (chromosomes, hormones, anatomy, typical development).
- Textbook-level variation (intersex conditions, DSDs, their prevalence and medical management).
- Social-science findings on gender identity, sexual orientation, behavior, family structure.
- Contested interpretations and policy debates — flagged as such.

## Why this book exists

Because this is central to being human, and because the current cultural moment is bad at distinguishing what is known from what is contested. A tier-strict treatment, refusing to collapse into either traditionalist dismissal or ideological certainty, is useful.
