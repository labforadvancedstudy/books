## What this book covers

The body as you experience it: the skin you're in, the pain you feel, the hunger and fatigue and pleasure, the body as medium through which you act on the world. Adjacent to `HA_life` (biology) and `HA_consciousness` (phenomenology of mind), this book focuses on the embodied dimension of being human.

## Why this book exists

Strip away metaphor and the body is where every experience happens. The old tree treated humans as cognitive agents; this book insists on humans as flesh that thinks.
