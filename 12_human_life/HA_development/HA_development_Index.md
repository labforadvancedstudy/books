## What this book covers

Human development across the lifespan: infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and senescence. What the body does, what the mind does, what the social self does at each stage. Critical periods and plasticity. The real timeline, not just the stereotypes.

## Why this book exists

A human is not a static object. The person at 5 and 25 and 75 are the same continuous self but fundamentally different in body, cognition, and social position. The old tree had psychology but no developmental psychology, had consciousness but no lifespan view of consciousness.
