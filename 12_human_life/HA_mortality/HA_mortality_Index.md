## What this book covers

Aging, dying, grief, and how humans navigate the end of life. The biology of senescence, the practice of end-of-life care, the psychology of dying and bereavement, death rituals across cultures, and the fundamental question of how to live in light of finitude.

## Why this book exists

Death is the universal human experience and one of the most avoided topics in polite modern discourse. A dedicated, honest treatment — distinct from either religious tradition or speculative longevity — is useful.
